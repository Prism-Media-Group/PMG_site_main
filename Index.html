What We Do

Media across the spectrum

From entertainment production to broadcasting infrastructure, we create, distribute, and amplify content that matters.

Entertainment Production
Original series, creator-driven content, and entertainment formats that celebrate diverse voices and LGBTQ+ stories.
Broadcasting & Streaming
Live broadcasting, radio, and streaming services reaching audiences across the UK, Europe, and beyond.
Creative & Branding
Visual identity, campaigns, and bold creative direction for brands that want to stand out and make an impact.
Archive & Preservation
Curating and preserving British television history, memorable moments, and cultural broadcasting heritage.
Community & Inclusion
Building platforms and spaces that centre LGBTQ+ voices, champion representation, and foster inclusive communities.
Partnerships & Collaboration
Working with creators, brands, and organisations on co-productions, cross-platform projects, and media partnerships.

Prism Media Group was formed in 2025 when four independent brands — Magic Plum Company, Femboy Media Corporation, United Broadcasting Company, and Anarchy Group — came together under one unified identity.

What we stand for

Everything we do is guided by a clear set of principles. We're not just a media group — we're a statement.

LGBTQ+ representation isn't a checkbox — it's our foundation. Every project, every frame, every story.
