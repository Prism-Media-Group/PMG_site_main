Our Origin & Evolution

Prism Media Group represents a new chapter in inclusive media and creative technology. Formed through the unified vision of three distinct industry trailblazers, our collective strength allows us to provide unmatched digital tools and content platforms tailored for marginalized voices.

The Legacy Merger: Three independent entities joined forces to form Prism Media Group:

  • Magic Plum Company
  • Anarchy Group
  • United Broadcasting Co.
↓

Rebranded into Prism Media Group

By bringing these teams under one banner, we combined expertise in creative digital design, media broadcasting, and software infrastructure to build a truly comprehensive network.

What Drives Us

Representation is more than just being visible—it is about ownership, creative freedom, and digital safety. We operate under three core principles:

1. Creator Ownership: Providing tools and platforms where queer creators own their content and control their narratives without systemic barriers.

2. Accessible Technology: Designing software solutions through Prism Technology that ensure safety, accessibility, and high performance for creators everywhere.

3. Seamless Ecosystem: Unifying digital access across all our services using MyPMGI, making user interaction smooth and centralized.

Authentic media and technology made by and for the LGBTQ+ community.
